false, abstention_reason_code to null, and emit every necessary typed component.

no_cutoff_eligible_state, insufficient_product_version_specificity,
predicate_absent, wrong_authority_for_predicate, or
unresolved_authoritative_evidence. Extra fields, malformed JSON, missing
components, incomplete citations, or an invalid abstention shape are scored
failures.
